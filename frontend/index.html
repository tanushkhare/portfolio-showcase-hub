Engineering Architecture & Standards

Every microservice in this 23-repository portfolio follows production architectural standards:

  • ACID & Relational State: Deprecated ephemeral in-memory lists in favor of transactional persistence patterns.
  • Defensive AST Sandboxing: Natural language compilers (such as Voice-to-SQL) enforce AST validation to ensure query safety.
  • Non-blocking I/O: High-throughput streaming and data pipelines decouple CPU-bound tasks with asynchronous threading.
  • Strict Verification: Zero tolerance for silent catch-all mocks or 404 route mismatches.
